Philosophy

  • I am a Hungarian literary translator working to bring Shakespeare’s plays closer to today’s readers and theatergoers. My approach blends fresh, contemporary Hungarian in dialogues with newly crafted rhymes for soliloquies—ensuring clarity, emotional resonance, and poetic flair for a younger audience. While Hungary has a proud tradition of Shakespeare translation—from János Arany to modern classics—these often use historic language that can feel distant and obscure to newer generations. My goal is to make these timeless works accessible, vivid, and moving for the 21st century. My translation of Macbeth is available at macbeth.hu, and I’m currently working on Hamlet.
